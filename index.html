How it works

A machine that does the form. Honestly.

01

The engine rates every runner

Around thirty evidence checks per horse — class against official ratings, form, track and distance records, the speed map, track bias, sectionals. The order is locked before the market is looked at, so the numbers are never bent to fit the odds.

02

Your picks are ready at midnight

Wake up to the full day: the Top 5 board, every race's pick with plain-English reasons, trifecta and first-four plays. Then the engine re-checks each race at 10, 5, 3 and 2 minutes out — the final word lands a minute before the jump, never later.

03

Results go up the same night

Every result and dividend, posted win or lose. The scoreboard is the whole pitch: if we're good, you'll see it. If we have a bad day, you'll see that too — at full size.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

What exactly do I get each day?

The full day's card for NZ and Australia: our Top 5 win bets, every race's pick with the reasoning in plain punter English, exotic plays where the race shape suits, and live results with dividends as they settle. Picks are on the app from midnight and stay fresh to a minute before each jump.

What's your strike rate, honestly?

It lives on the scoreboard in the app, updated every day — typically around 30% winners on top picks, with the AU board carrying the win value lately. We publish the losing days at the same size as the winning ones, which is exactly why you can trust the winning ones.

How do I know the results aren't massaged?

The pick that counts is the one published before the race jumped — and that's the one the scoreboard settles against. Our engine keeps an internal audit that reconciles every published pick against every result, and races lock permanently the moment they've run.

Is this betting advice?

No. Our selections are opinions based on evidence — not guarantees and not financial advice. Racing involves risk. Set a budget, stick to it, and never stake more than you can afford to lose.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. No contracts, no minimum term, no hoops. If we're not earning your $25 a week, you shouldn't be paying it.

Who's behind The Collect?

The Collect Limited, New Zealand — two partners: one who built the engine, one who spent decades doing the form the hard way. The engine is the automation of that craft, tested against nearly two thousand settled races before a single pick went public.
