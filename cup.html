What the number means

The rating is a comparison inside this race, and nothing more. The top-rated runner is set at 100 and everything else is scaled against it, so you can see how far apart they are rather than just the order they came in.

It is not a probability. It is not a prediction. It is the engine saying: on the evidence available this morning, this is how these twenty-four horses stack up against each other.

The Cup is a 3,200 metre handicap with a capacity field. It is, on any honest reading, one of the hardest races of the year to rate. We have published the card anyway, because publishing only the easy ones would make the scoreboard worthless.

Why we've put this up for nothing

Because on Tuesday night we publish the result against it.

Every selection the engine has made since August is already public, winners and losers at the same size, at thecollect.nz/results. This card goes on the same pile. If it reads the race well, that's on the record. If it misses, that's on the record too, in the same type.

That's the whole business. Everyone in racing claims winners. Almost nobody publishes the rest.
