Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026 · The Collect, New Zealand

The short version

We collect as close to nothing as a service can. The Collect is built so your picks, results and settings live on your own device.

What we collect

Right now: nothing personally identifying. The app stores your access code status, theme choice and settings in your browser's local storage — on your device, never sent to us. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) keeps standard, short-lived server logs (IP address, pages requested) to run the service, as every website's host does.

When paid subscriptions launch: your email address and subscription status, plus payment handled entirely by our payment provider (such as Stripe, Apple or Google) — we never see or store your card details.

What we never do

We don't sell or share your information with anyone for marketing. We don't track your betting. We don't run advertising or third-party trackers in the app.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you may ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Since we currently hold none, deletion is as simple as clearing the app from your device — but you're welcome to ask.
