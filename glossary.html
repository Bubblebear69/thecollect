Glossary

Racing, in plain English.

Every term you'll see on The Collect, explained the way you'd explain it to a mate. No jargon for the sake of it.

The Collect's terms

Best bet

The selections we rate most strongly on the day — where our evidence and the market point the same way. We only flag a handful, and only when the case is at its strongest.

Collect Rating

Our 0–100 strength score for each of the main contenders in a race. It's scaled within that one race — the runner we rate hardest sits at or near 100, the rest fall in behind — so you can see how far clear our top picks are of each other. A guide to our confidence, not a win percentage and not a price.

Top 5 board

Our leading runners in a race, in order — the pick, the main dangers and the each-way hopes, each with its Collect Rating.

Main danger & each-way hope

The labels under the pick on the board. The main danger is the next-best runner that most threatens it; an each-way hope is a place-chance type worth an each-way play.

Top 3 chance

Our read on a runner's chance of finishing in the first three — separate from its chance of winning, so you can weigh a place or exotic play.

Top 4 chance

The same idea for a top-four finish — handy for first-four and each-way thinking.

Confidence grade

The A+ to C grade we put on a pick — how strong we think the overall case is.

Confidence band

A trust badge for the race, showing how much weight to put on our read based on how deep and clean the form is. It's about the data behind the call, not how good the tip is.

Roughie of the day

Our best big-priced play — a selection we're confident in that's carrying juicy odds.

Firming (money coming)

When a runner's price has shortened sharply since our first look — a sign of real market support.

Tip of the Day

One selection we publish free every day — a taste of what members get. On the site it's the pick from our Free Race of the Day.

Strike rate

The share of our selections that won over a stated period — say, how many of our top picks saluted this week. Taken straight from our published results, counting winners only.

Spread trifecta / first four

A wider exotic suggestion — the runners to box together, plus the next ones to add if you want to widen the net. We give you the numbers, not the maths.

The Engine's Blackbook

A watch-list of horses worth following next start — ones we rated highly that ran better than the bare result shows: unlucky, caught by a track bias, or storming home at a big price. A pointer for next time, never a tip on a past race.

Scratching

A runner withdrawn before the race. We still show scratched runners, struck through — never quietly dropped. We publish everything. If our top pick is scratched, the whole race is voided — shown struck through with the field, but not counted toward any of our win, place or exotic strike rates.

Racing terms, plainly

Win and place

A win bet pays only if your runner finishes first. A place bet pays if it finishes in the first three (first two in smaller fields) — less to collect, but it comes in more often.

Each-way

One bet in two parts — a win bet and a place bet, so it's two stakes. You collect on both if it wins, and just the place part if it only runs a place.

Trifecta

Picking the first three home in the correct order. Harder than a win bet, but it pays a lot more when it lands.

First four

Picking the first four home in the correct order — a step up again from a trifecta, with a bigger dividend to match.

Starting price (SP)

The odds a runner is at when the race jumps. Our scoreboard settles on real prices like these, never cherry-picked ones.

Dividend

What a winning bet pays for every $1 staked, as declared by the TAB once the race is official.

Favourite

The runner the market rates most likely to win — the shortest price in the race. Often right, regularly beaten, which is where our edge has to come from.
