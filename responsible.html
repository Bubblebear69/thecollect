Responsible Gambling

The Collect is for people 18 and over, full stop.

Our position

Punting should be entertainment paid for out of money you can afford to lose — never the rent, never the groceries, never borrowed. Our picks are researched opinions, not certainties: even a good method has losing days and losing weeks. If betting has stopped being fun, stop betting — no scoreboard is worth more than your wellbeing.

Keep yourself safe

Set a weekly limit before you start and never chase losses. Don't bet when you're upset, drunk or trying to fix a bad day. Take breaks. Tell someone if it's getting on top of you. The TAB offers deposit limits and self-exclusion tools — use them early, not late.
