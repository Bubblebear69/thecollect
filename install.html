Put The Collect on your home screen

It opens like an app — the same thing you see in the browser, with its own icon. There's nothing to download and no app store.

iPhone & iPad

Safari or Chrome on iOS 16.4+

  1. Open thecollect.nz/app in Safari (on iOS 16.4 or later you can also use Chrome or Edge).
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up — at the bottom of the screen (top-right in Chrome).
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top corner. The Collect icon appears on your home screen.

Safari is the simplest path and works on every version. On iOS 16.4 and later, Chrome and Edge can add to the home screen too.

Android

Chrome

  1. Open thecollect.nz/app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Install app — on some phones it says Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install (or Add). The Collect icon appears on your home screen.

If you don't see the option, make sure you're in Chrome and the page has finished loading, then open the ⋮ menu again.
