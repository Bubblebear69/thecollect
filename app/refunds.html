Refund Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026 · The Collect, New Zealand

The Collect is a weekly subscription you can cancel any time. Here is exactly how refunds work — kept short on purpose.

You start free

Every member starts on a free trial — 7 days for a standard new sign-up, or a free first month for founding members and anyone who joins during December 2026. You add a card to begin, but nothing is charged until the free period ends. Cancel before it ends and you are never charged, so there is nothing to refund.

Cancelling a paid membership

Membership is $25 per week, billed weekly in advance. Cancel any time from your account and you keep access until the end of the week you have already paid for — we do not bill you again after that. We bill in whole weeks and do not refund part-weeks, because one week is the smallest amount we ever charge.

No refunds based on results

Our picks are researched opinions, and racing carries real risk. Losing days and losing weeks happen to every method — they are part of the game, not a fault in the service. We do not give refunds on the basis of results, a losing week, or bets that did not win.

If we don't deliver

If we fail to publish picks on a normal race day, that is on us. Tell us and we will extend your access or refund that week's fee. Where the service genuinely has not worked as described, we will put it right.

Billing mistakes

Charged twice, charged after you cancelled, or billed in error? Email us with the details and we will sort it promptly — that is a mistake to fix, not a judgement call.

Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits the rights you have under the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and Fair Trading Act 1986 that cannot lawfully be excluded. This policy sits alongside those rights; it does not replace them.

How to ask

Email hello@thecollect.nz with your account email and what happened, and we will come back to you.
